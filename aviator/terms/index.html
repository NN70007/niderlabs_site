1. What the App does

The App displays on a compatible Garmin watch a summary of publicly available weather reports (METAR) for airports you choose, together with values computed from them (for example wind components, flight category, density altitude, sunrise and sunset). The data come from third parties – in particular aviationweather.gov (NOAA) – through your paired phone.
