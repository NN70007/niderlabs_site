Privacy Policy

Draft – version of 5 November 2026. Controller: NiderLabs (see the legal notice).

In short

  • No account, no name, no e-mail address, no payment data.
  • Your precise position never leaves your watch and phone: it is only used on the watch to find the nearest airport.
  • We keep a random watch identifier and your settings, so that the settings page can reach your watch.

1. Data we process

  • Watch identifier: a random number created by the App on your watch (not your Garmin serial number or account), and a fingerprint of a random key – the key itself is never stored.
  • Settings: airports (ICAO codes), runways, units, crosswind limit, magnetic variation, date format, map preference.
  • Technical data: date of first and last contact, App version, the date you accepted the terms.
  • Remembered phone (only if you tick “Remember this phone”): a random key kept in your browser for 30 days; we store only its fingerprint and expiry date. “Forget this phone”, “Forget all phones” or signing out deletes it.

Weather data are requested directly from aviationweather.gov (NOAA) by the Garmin Connect IQ app on your phone; those requests are subject to NOAA's and Garmin's own privacy policies.

2. Why, and on what legal basis

To provide the service you ask for – linking the Site to your watch and sending it your settings (performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) – and, if you leave the option ticked, to show airports anonymously on the public map (consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, which you can withdraw at any time by unticking the option).

3. The public map

The map shows airports set on watches seen in the last 30 days, with the number of watches rounded into bands (1–4, 5 or more, 10 or more). No identifier, date or other detail is published.

4. Where the data are kept, and for how long

The data are stored in a private Google Sheets file operated by NiderLabs on Google Workspace / Google Apps Script (Google acts as processor; data may be processed outside the EU under Google's standard contractual clauses). Records of watches not seen for 12 months are deleted.

5. Your rights

You may ask for access, correction or deletion of the data linked to your watch, or object to their processing, by writing to the contact in the legal notice and giving the code shown on your watch. You may also lodge a complaint with the Luxembourg data protection authority (CNPD, cnpd.public.lu).

6. Cookies

The Site sets no cookies. It only remembers your day/night mode choice in your browser. The map uses map tiles from OpenStreetMap and the pages use fonts from Google Fonts, which receive your IP address when loading them.
