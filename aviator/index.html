Questions

Good to know.

How much does it cost?

Free forever if you install before 31 December 2026 – yours to keep. From 1 January 2027: $9.99, one-time, no subscription. The Crosswind METAR App is free for owners of the watch face.

Watch face or app – which one do I need?

The watch face, if your watch has a touch screen. The Crosswind METAR App is for watches with buttons only: it shows the same pages and is free for owners of the watch face.

My watch has no touch screen. Does it work?

Yes. On the fēnix 6 series, install the watch face: it shows the setup QR code until you have set it up, then the normal dial. The dial always shows your airport 1: to show another one, put it first in the settings on this page. Add the Crosswind METAR App for the runway and METAR pages (UP and DOWN) and to look at your other airports (START).

Does it need a connection?

Your watch gets the METAR through your phone (Bluetooth), every few minutes. Without your phone nearby, the watch keeps the last report and shows how old it is.

Can I use it to fly?

No. Crosswind METAR is for situational awareness only – NOT FOR NAVIGATION. Always get an official weather briefing; the pilot in command remains responsible for every decision.
