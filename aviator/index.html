Privacy & security

Your airports. Nothing else.

To put your airports on your watch, we have to know which airports you chose. That is the only thing about you we keep – no name, no e-mail, no Garmin account, no phone number, no payment details.

Read the privacy policy →

01
What we keep, and why

A random number your watch makes up for itself, your settings (airports, runways, units, crosswind limit) and the date the watch last called in. With that, this page can send your settings to your watch, and the watch can show each airport's local time.

02
Your position stays on your wrist

The watch uses it only to find the nearest airport. If you use NEAREST, we receive that airport's code – ELLX, for example – never your coordinates.

03
The weather does not go through us

Your watch asks NOAA's aviationweather.gov directly, through your phone. We never see the weather you read.

04
No account to hack

No password exists. The 6-digit code on your watch works for a few minutes, for your watch only, and keys are stored only as fingerprints.

05
The map is optional

If you leave the option ticked, your airports appear on the public map, counted in rounded bands, with no identifier. Untick it and they never will.

06
Gone when you stop

A watch silent for 12 months is erased. Want it sooner? Write to us with the code your watch shows.

Questions

Good to know.

How much does it cost?

Free forever if you install before 31 December 2026 – yours to keep. From 1 January 2027: $9.99, one-time, no subscription. The Aviator METAR Pro App is free for owners of the watch face.

Watch face or app – which one do I need?

The watch face, if your watch has a touch screen. The Aviator METAR Pro App is for watches with buttons only: it shows the same pages and is free for owners of the watch face.

My watch has no touch screen. Does it work?

Yes. On the fēnix 6 series, install the watch face: it shows the setup QR code until you have set it up, then the normal dial. The dial always shows your airport 1: to show another one, put it first in the settings on this page. Add the Aviator METAR Pro App for the runway and METAR pages (UP and DOWN) and to look at your other airports (START).

Does it need a connection?

Your watch gets the METAR through your phone (Bluetooth), every few minutes. Without your phone nearby, the watch keeps the last report and shows how old it is.

Can I use it to fly?

No. Aviator METAR Pro is for situational awareness only – NOT FOR NAVIGATION. Always get an official weather briefing; the pilot in command remains responsible for every decision.
