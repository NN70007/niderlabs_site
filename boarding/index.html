Link your watch

  1. Tap the Boarding watch face: ADD A FLIGHT appears.
  2. Scan its QR code with your phone camera, or type the 6-digit code here.

Save this page to your bookmarks (or add it to your home screen) to come back in one tap.

No account, no password. The code only works for a few minutes and only for your watch.

My flights

Add a flight

Point the camera at the barcode of your boarding pass (paper, airline app or wallet).

Your boarding pass is read on this phone: only the flight number, date, airports and seat are sent – never your name.

Followed flights

Alerts on this phone

Gate change, delay, cancellation, boarding and baggage belt: your phone is notified, and your Garmin watch vibrates with the phone notification.

iPhone: first add this page to your home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen), open it from there, then tap “Turn on alerts”.
